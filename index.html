THE MISSION

One person. One baseline.
A clearer picture of change.

ASTRA HEALTH organises health readings around each astronaut's individual Earth reference. It helps authorised teams review trends and unusual statistical deviations with mission and activity context.

01

Personal baselines

Build a reference from at least five valid Earth RESTING readings, then submit it for medical review.

02

Health monitoring

Record heart rate, SpO₂ and blood pressure with activity, mission phase and data-quality context.

03

Offline-first

Queue readings locally during a connection interruption and synchronise after reconnection.

04

Review & analytics

Explore trends, statistical deviations, audit history and downloadable research summaries.
